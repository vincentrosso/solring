How it works

One person asks. One person helps. Beatrix sits between them: it answers what it can, turns the rest into a clear to-do list, and never claims a job is done when it is not. The AI cannot send email, book, buy or call. People do that.

Privacy

  • What is stored: the messages you write, the replies, and the task list. Nothing else: no contacts, no location, no photos.
  • Who can read it: you, your assistant, and the person who runs the service.
  • Where it lives: on Cloudflare's servers, which also run the AI model that writes Beatrix's replies. Your messages are sent to that model to produce an answer.
  • What we do not do: no ads, no analytics or tracking, no selling or sharing of your data.
  • Sign-in: no accounts, passwords or codes. Your device keeps a random key, and the person who runs the service decides which devices are let in.
  • Deleting: ask the person who invited you and everything you wrote is erased.

Help

This is a small private service. For a problem, a new phone, or to have your data removed, contact the person who invited you.
